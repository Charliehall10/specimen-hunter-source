## Decisions made
- **Route:** real-money B2B. Realistic £0 path: build a polished portfolio game, then use it to get a job, freelance work, or pitch a licensed studio. Own supplier licence only after income or a funding partner.
- **Why not straight to market:** UKGC licence, independent test-house certification and operator/aggregator onboarding all cost money and months.
- **Theme:** UK coarse/carp fishing. Working title: Specimen Hunter (placeholder, trademark NOT yet checked).
- **v1 free spins:** simple collect (fish values summed each spin). Venue-upgrade ladder parked for v2.
- **Signature mechanic: "Three Rods".** Bite alarm is the scatter, only on reels 2, 3, 4. One alarm on each of the three reels triggers 10 free spins. Three alarms during free spins add 5 spins. (Inspired by the 3-rod licence; check current EA licence rules before any marketing use.)

## Game spec v0.1
- 5x3 grid, 10 fixed paylines, line pays left to right, wild substitutes (not for scatter or cash fish)
- Symbols: low (float, boilie, corn, pellet), mid (flask, catapult, net, bivvy), high (roach, bream, tench, mirror carp), wild (leather carp), scatter (bite alarm), cash fish (free spins only)
- Free spins: fish land with weights (lb/oz) that map to fixed bet multiples; all fish on screen are collected each spin
- Record fish: 52lb 0oz = 250x, 64lb 8oz = 1000x
- UK design rules built in: no autoplay, no turbo, no slam stop, 2.5s minimum spin, returns below stake are not celebrated, session net shown

## Math results (500,000,000 spins, simulator seed 5)
- RTP: 96.125% (95% CI +/- 0.063%). Base game 54.1%, free spins 42.0%
- Hit frequency: 25.49%
- Returns below 1x stake: 11.15% of spins
- Feature frequency: 1 in 152 (exact strip maths: 1 in 151.7)
- Average free spins per feature: 10.34. Average feature win: 63.7x bet
- Volatility (std dev): 7.21
- Max win observed: 2,117.6x. Wins of 1,000x+ about 1 in 77,000 spins. 5,000x cap hit: 0 times
- Mean fish value: 3.052x bet
