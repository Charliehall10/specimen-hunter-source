The kingfisher: a base-game surprise

Now and then

About 1 base spin in 300, a kingfisher flies in while the reels are still spinning.

A row of wilds

It dives into reels 2, 3 and 4 on one row, plus up to two more spots, turning each into a Leather Carp wild.

Always worth it

That row always pays at least four of a kind. A visit averages 8.9× stake.

6

The base game needs moments between bonuses. The kingfisher gives one roughly every 12 minutes of play, and because it always takes a full row on reels 2 to 4 it never ends in nothing. A Bite Alarm in a chosen spot is left alone, so the free-spins trigger rate does not change.
