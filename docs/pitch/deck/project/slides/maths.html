The PAR sheet works out line-win probabilities with live Excel formulas from the strip symbol counts, then checks them against an independent calculation from the engine's rules. The two agree exactly. The baited swims are worked out exactly too, by stepping through every possible feature. A record fish in a deep swim can pass the 5,000x cap (about 1 in 6 million rounds), so the cap's cost is measured separately and taken off.
