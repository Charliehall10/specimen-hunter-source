Three Rods: the trigger

One alarm per rod

A bite alarm on each of reels 2, 3 and 4 in the same spin wins 10 free spins. That happens about 1 in 152 spins.

Anticipation built in

With two rods lit, reels 4 and 5 spin 1.2 seconds longer under a red glow. Spins only ever get longer, never shorter.

Retriggers

Three alarms again during free spins add 5 more.

The three lights above the reels are the rod pod. Each one lights up as its alarm lands, with a rising beep, so players can see the trigger building one reel at a time.
