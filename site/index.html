Specimen Hunter

A British carp-fishing slot built around one moment every angler knows: three bite alarms going off at once.

The idea

Carp anglers fish with three rods, each with an electronic bite alarm. When all three go at once, it's chaos and joy. In Specimen Hunter that moment is the bonus trigger: a bite alarm on each of reels 2, 3 and 4 starts the Three Rods free spins.

Before the free spins start, a catapult fires bait into three swims on the reels. Specimen Fish land with real weights, from 2lb 4oz up to a record 64lb 8oz, and pay the moment they land. A fish caught in a baited swim pays ×2, and that swim's multiplier doubles with every catch, up to ×32.

In the base game, now and then a kingfisher flies in while the reels spin and dives into the water, turning a row of reels 2 to 4 (and sometimes more) into wild Leather Carp.

The moments

Caught in a swim

Every fish shows its weight and pays at once. In a baited swim it's multiplied, and the swim doubles: here a 14lb 0oz fish lands at ×32 for £160.

Baiting the swims

Before the first free spin a catapult fires boilies into three swims, which stay put for the whole feature.

The kingfisher

Now and then in the base game it dives in and turns a whole row of reels 2 to 4 wild, so it always brings a win.

Big Catch tiers

Big, Huge and Monster Catch at 15×, 50× and 200×, before the feature summary.

Record fish

52lb 0oz pays 250× and 64lb 8oz pays 1,000× stake.

How it's built

One engine, two places. The same tested engine file decides outcomes in the browser demo and on a dependency-free game server, which handles sessions, stake caps, a whole-pence wallet and safe retries.

Everything original. Symbols, scenery, music and sound are generated in code with PixiJS and Web Audio: no licensed assets.

Checked. 20 automated tests, and a formula-driven PAR sheet that matches the engine exactly.
